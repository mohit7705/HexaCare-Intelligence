🧠 HexaCare Intelligence

AI-Powered 6-in-1 Early Health Screening Platform

HexaCare Intelligence is an AI-driven healthcare intelligence platform designed to provide early health risk screening using Machine Learning, Large Language Models, Vision AI, and secure blockchain-backed data handling.
The platform integrates six critical health modules into a single, easy-to-use system, enabling preventive care, awareness, and early intervention.

🚀 Problem Statement

Million of people lack access to:

Early disease detection

Preventive healthcare insights

Affordable medical screening

Mental health awareness

Traditional healthcare systems are reactive, expensive, and fragmented.

HexaCare Intelligence solves this by offering proactive, AI-powered health screening in one unified platform.

💡 Solution Overview

HexaCare Intelligence provides six AI-based health screening tools in one platform:

AI Symptom Checker

Mental Health Insight Engine

Diabetes Risk Prediction

Heart Disease Risk Analysis

Skin Disease Detection (Vision AI)

Women’s Health Screening (PCOS & related conditions)

The platform is designed for:

Early risk awareness (not diagnosis)

Preventive healthcare

Scalability for hospitals, NGOs, and individuals

🧩 Core Features
🔹 1. AI Symptom Checker

Uses LLMs to analyze user-entered symptoms

Provides possible conditions and next-step guidance

Conversational and user-friendly interface

🔹 2. Mental Health Analysis

Evaluates stress, anxiety, and depression indicators

Uses NLP-based questionnaires

Offers supportive insights and coping suggestions

🔹 3. Diabetes Prediction

ML model trained on medical datasets

Predicts diabetes risk based on health parameters

Fast and lightweight inference

🔹 4. Heart Disease Risk Assessment

Predicts cardiovascular risk

Uses clinical features such as age, cholesterol, BP, etc.

Helps users take early preventive steps

🔹 5. Skin Disease Detection

Vision AI model for skin condition analysis

Accepts uploaded images

Detects common skin issues (informational purpose only)

🔹 6. Women’s Health Screening

PCOS-related risk screening

Focused on awareness and trend analysis

Dataset-driven AI insights
